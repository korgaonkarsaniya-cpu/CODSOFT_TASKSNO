ABOUT MAHARASHTRA

A Land of History, Nature & Culture

Maharashtra is a diverse state known for its rich history, beautiful landscapes, ancient forts, beaches and vibrant culture.

From the busy streets of Mumbai to the peaceful beaches of Konkan and the green hills of the Western Ghats, Maharashtra offers something for every traveler.
